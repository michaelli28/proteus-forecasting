### C-GEO-1 — Elite Defection Cascade (coup-window forecasting)

- **Pitch (1 sentence):** Simulates the two dozen people who could actually remove a head of state — guard commanders, the internal-security chief, ruling-party barons — and reports the probability that enough of them move together inside a named six-month window, plus which single defection would move that probability most.
- **The analyst question it answers:** "What is the probability that [country]'s incumbent leaves office irregularly — coup, forced resignation under military pressure, palace removal — between 1 Jan and 30 Jun 2027, and which one actor's defection swings that probability most?" Resolvable against the Powell–Thyne / Cline Center coding of irregular leader exit inside the window.
- **Mission and user:** CIA Directorate of Analysis regional mission-center leadership-analysis cell; DIA regional J2; NIC regional NIO. **Today:** a branch chief writes a leadership memo off clandestine reporting plus a hand-maintained "who's up / who's down" chart of the security elite. The base rate comes from a colleague's recollection. There is no probability, no falsifier, no re-run when a new name enters the picture, and no record of what the last call was.
- **What the N simulants are:** N = 40–80 runs. Each run instantiates a coalition graph over 8–20 *named* pivotal actors. Per-actor state variables: private grievance stock `g_i`, defect-vs-stay payoff `u_i`, belief about the mass of others who will move `p_i`, purge/retaliation cost `c_i`, and unit-control weight `w_i` (share of coercive capacity actually commanded). Regime-level state: coup-proofing intensity, purge tempo, counter-balancing (number of parallel security organs), patronage liquidity. **riskTolerance** sets each actor's action threshold under signal noise; **escalationPropensity** sets whether an attempt is coded as quiet removal/arrest or armed seizure. Across runs the *leader's* counter-coup profile and the elite's information structure (rumor precision) are what vary — that is the mutually exclusive hypothesis space.
- **What the deterministic kernel computes:** A **global game / threshold-coordination model on a weighted network** (Morris–Shin style incomplete information over regime weakness θ; Granovetter thresholds for the action rule). Each actor draws a noisy private signal from the seeded PRNG, acts iff signal exceeds a threshold set by riskTolerance and expected mover-mass; best-response dynamics iterate to fixed point; seizure succeeds iff defecting `w`-mass clears a control threshold. Wrapped in a **discrete-time hazard** over the 180-day window with a coup-proofing decay term. Output includes ∂P/∂threshold for every named actor — the sensitivity table *is* the deliverable, not the scalar.
- **What the judge panel is:** ACH over five mutually exclusive bands (no irregular exit / failed attempt / successful military seizure / palace-or-party removal / preemptive purge leaving incumbent in place). Judges: (1) **ACH diagnosticity judge** — scores which reported item actually discriminates between bands versus merely being consistent with all of them, ranking by weighted inconsistency ascending; (2) **key-assumptions judge** — the pivotal-actor roster is itself the load-bearing assumption and must carry a falsifier ("if the Republican Guard commander is replaced, this model is void"); (3) **devil's advocate** — argues the null from the global coup base rate; (4) **premortem**; (5) **red team** with standing to force a bounded rerun when roster currency fails. Rubric axes: diagnosticity, base-rate discipline, roster currency, falsifiability per assumption, provenance completeness.
- **The config pack:** `actors/<country>-elite-roster.yaml` (named pivotals, control weights, patronage edges, per-node sourcing and as-of date); `kernel/coup-globalgame.json` (threshold priors, signal precision, coup-proofing decay, success mass); `rubric/coup-ach.yaml`; `adapter/leader-exit-domain.ts` (maps kernel bands onto Powell–Thyne exit categories so scoring is mechanical); `thresholds/abstain.yaml` (abstain if fewer than 60% of roster nodes have sourcing dated after the as-of line).
- **Data required:** **Unclassified:** Powell & Thyne global coup list, Cline Center Coup d'État Project, Archigos leader data, V-Dem / Regimes-of-the-World, ACLED, POLECAT weekly event data (the ICEWS successor; ICEWS itself was discontinued 11 Apr 2023), national gazettes, military promotion/retirement announcements, order-of-battle OSINT. **Genuinely classified:** loyalty/grievance signal on individual named officers, and any indication of active plotting. **Credible unclassified demo: YES** — a full Sahel/West Africa demo runs on open data alone; classified reporting changes node priors, not the kernel.
- **How accuracy is MEASURED:** (i) **Historical backtest, hard as-of cutoff** on coded episodes — Mali Aug 2020 and May 2021, Guinea Sep 2021, Sudan Oct 2021, Burkina Faso Jan and Sep 2022, Niger Jul 2023, Gabon Aug 2023, Bolivia's failed attempt Jun 2024 — with matched negative controls (elevated-risk country-halves with no exit). Metric: Brier and **Brier skill against two explicit baselines** — the global coup base rate, and the public Ward/Beger-style random-forest coup forecasts at predictiveheuristics.com. (ii) **Live post-cutoff:** standing 180-day questions on a named watchlist, resolved by Cline Center/Powell coding. **Leakage honesty:** parametric memorization of 2020–2023 coups is *not* fixed by as-of retrieval; backtest numbers are an upper bound, and only the live arm is evidence-class (c). The evaluation-regime tag enforces that the two never pool.
- **Benefit categories hit:** **(a)** the pivotal-actor sensitivity table is the artifact an analyst hand-builds over days; **(b)** rerun in minutes on a roster change vs. a memo cycle; **(c)** the ACH matrix names which HUMINT item is diagnostic versus merely consistent and flags where two streams disagree about the same officer; **(d)** roster refresh + rerun is the repetitive task automated.
- **Prior art and incumbents:** *Government:* the **Political Instability Task Force**, CIA-funded, two-year-horizon instability forecasting — the summary literature claims 80–90% accuracy, which is an accuracy-rate claim, not a skill score, and should be treated skeptically in the white paper. *Commercial:* **Rhombus Power "Guardian"** (reported US Air Force IDIQ, ceiling $200M, work through Sept 2030); Verisk Maplecroft / RANE country-risk subscriptions (product detail **UNVERIFIED**).
- **Failure modes and red lines:** *Technical:* the roster is the model — a stale roster yields a confidently wrong answer, hence roster currency as an abstention trigger. The kernel is prone to bimodal all-defect/none-defect output that inflates apparent sharpness; sharpness is a reported metric so both hedging and over-sharpness are visible. *Ethical/legal:* this names foreign officials and sits one step from coup-support planning. **Structural enforcement:** the domain adapter has no recommended-action field and the config schema has no "influence" or "effect" object, so an action product is not *representable*; a US-persons filter rejects any actor node resolving to a US person; outputs are typed `Assessment` with no code path to a targeting product.
- **TRL now / TRL in 12 months:** **4 / 6.** Kernel and panel run today on synthetic worlds; no coup backtest has been executed. Twelve months buys the full open-source backtest under the as-of harness plus a live watchlist arm on a government network.

### C-GEO-2 — Mobilization Cascade and Security-Force Defection

- **Pitch (1 sentence):** Takes a protest wave that has already started and forecasts whether it grows into the kind of movement security forces stop shooting at, including what happens if the regime cuts the internet.
- **The analyst question it answers:** "The wave that began in [country] on [date]: what is the probability that peak 30-day ACLED protest-event count lands in each of the bands {<200, 200–999, 1,000–4,999, ≥5,000} within 90 days, and conditional on the top two bands, that at least one uniformed unit publicly refuses orders?"
- **Mission and user:** ODNI/NIC current-intelligence, State INR, CIA regional analysis line. **Today:** analysts count crowd photographs and social-media clips, write "protests continue," and escalate on gut feel; nobody separates "large but fizzling" from "small but cascading," which are the two states that actually differ.
- **What the N simulants are:** N = 60–100 runs over a population network with threshold-heterogeneous agents. State variables: private opposition preference `θ_i`, revealed-participation threshold `τ_i` (Kuran-style preference falsification), local observed turnout, repression exposure, economic shock (fuel/bread price), regime concession stock, and per-unit security-force cohesion. **riskTolerance** governs how much observed turnout a citizen needs before revealing preference; **escalationPropensity** governs the regime's repression response function and whether it escalates to lethal force. Run-to-run variation is over three mutually exclusive regime strategies (concede / repress hard early / repress late) crossed with two information regimes (internet on / shut down) — that is a genuine, decision-relevant hypothesis space, not cosmetic noise.
- **What the deterministic kernel computes:** **Epidemiological/diffusion (threshold-SIR) on a spatial-social network**, coupled to (i) a repression-response function with a **backfire** parameter (repression raises `θ` for observers while lowering turnout locally) and (ii) a **security-force defection sub-model** solving each unit commander's payoff of firing versus defecting as a function of crowd size and perceived regime survival probability — a second, nested coordination threshold. Seeded PRNG for the threshold draws; deterministic iteration to fixed point or to horizon.
- **What the judge panel is:** ACH over the four count bands plus the defection binary. Judges: **diagnosticity** (which indicator separates cascade from fizzle — strike participation and elite-defection statements, not crowd-photo counts); **key-assumptions** (each with a falsifier: "assumes bazaar/merchant strike is a leading indicator — falsified if strike occurs with no subsequent count increase"); **devil's advocate** arguing regime survival, which is the historical mode; **premortem**; **red team** on whether the network structure was assumed rather than measured. Rubric axes: leading-indicator diagnosticity, base-rate discipline on regime survival, network-assumption grounding, falsifier quality, provenance.
- **The config pack:** `actors/<country>-mobilization.yaml` (population strata, threshold distributions, strike-capable sectors, security-force order of battle with cohesion priors); `kernel/mobilization-sir.json` (contagion rate, backfire coefficient, decay, defection payoff matrix); `rubric/protest-ach.yaml`; `adapter/acled-protest-domain.ts` (band boundaries defined in ACLED event-count terms so resolution is mechanical); `thresholds/abstain.yaml` (abstain when ACLED coverage for the country is below a coverage floor, which is the real failure mode in closed states).
- **Data required:** **Unclassified:** ACLED, Mass Mobilization Data (Clark & Regan), NAVCO 2.1 (389 campaigns, 1945–2013, Chenoweth & Shay), POLECAT, GDELT, IODA/NetBlocks internet-shutdown data, national strike reporting, FX/black-market rate series. **Classified:** security-force unit cohesion, regime internal deliberations. **Unclassified demo: YES.**
- **How accuracy is MEASURED:** **Historical backtest with as-of cutoff at wave onset** — positives: Sudan Dec 2018, Belarus Aug 2020, Kazakhstan Jan 2022, Sri Lanka Mar 2022, Iran Sep 2022, Bangladesh Jul 2024. Negatives (waves that fizzled or were contained): Iran Nov 2019, Russia Jan 2021. Metric: Brier and Brier skill vs. (1) the unconditional escalation base rate and (2) a logistic on lagged ACLED protest counts — the honest floor. **Live post-cutoff:** register 90-day band questions at the onset of any new wave; resolution is a public ACLED query. Add coverage@N and tail recall, because the value here is catching the rare cascade, not the common fizzle.
- **Benefit categories hit:** **(b)** collapses a multi-day mobilization assessment into an hours-long run with an explicit indicator list to watch; **(c)** reconciles social-media-derived crowd estimates against ACLED coding and press reporting, and reports where they contradict rather than averaging them away; **(a)** the I&W list is generated with falsifiers attached; **(d)** the wave-monitoring rerun cadence is automated.
- **Prior art and incumbents:** *Government:* **ACLED CAST** (hierarchical LGBM forecasts of organized violence, publicly documented methodology and code); PITF. *Academic:* Chenoweth's NAVCO program and the "3.5%" participation threshold literature. *Commercial:* Dataminr and Recorded Future for real-time event alerting (their forecasting claims **UNVERIFIED**).
- **Failure modes and red lines:** *Technical:* threshold-diffusion models are notoriously sensitive to the assumed network structure, which is usually unmeasured — this is where the model will silently be wrong, and it is why network-assumption grounding is a rubric axis rather than a footnote. Closed-state ACLED undercounting biases both input and oracle in the same direction, which can manufacture apparent skill. *Ethical/legal:* modeling protest dynamics is adjacent to protest suppression and to influence operations. **Structural enforcement:** the config schema exposes no lever for messaging, narrative, or population targeting; there is no output type representing an intervention; a US-persons filter blocks any run whose population stratum resolves to a US domestic population, and the domain adapter refuses to load a US-domestic geography.
- **TRL now / TRL in 12 months:** **4 / 6.**

### C-GEO-3 — Coercion Tempo Ladder (PRC gray-zone pressure on Taiwan)

- **The analyst question it answers:** "Over the next 180 days, in how many days will PLA aircraft crossings of the Taiwan Strait median line exceed 20 (bands: 0–10 / 11–30 / 31–60 / 61–120 / >120 days), and what is the probability of a first announced quarantine or 'inspection' regime on Taiwan-bound commercial shipping?"
- **Mission and user:** INDOPACOM J2 and J5; ONI; DIA China Mission Group; the NIC's East Asia NIO. **Today:** a watch officer maintains a daily tally from Taiwan MND press releases and CSIS ChinaPower's ADIZ dataset, and the assessment says "sustained elevated pressure." There is no distribution, no probability on discrete rungs, and no way to score last quarter's language.
- **What the N simulants are:** N = 30–60 runs. State variables: PRC coercion budget (sorties, hulls, days), reputational commitment stock (a ratchet — a rung taken cannot be cheaply abandoned), Taiwan response posture, US declaratory and physical posture, PRC domestic-calendar constraints (plenum, Party congress, Two Sessions), typhoon/seasonal flyability, and a Hawkes intensity state. **riskTolerance** sets PRC willingness to accept incident risk per rung; **escalationPropensity** sets the ladder-climb rate. Runs are parameterized by mutually exclusive PRC strategy profiles: *normalize-and-habituate*, *punish-on-trigger*, *pre-position-for-quarantine*, *de-escalate-for-economic-relief*.
- **What the deterministic kernel computes:** A **self-exciting point process (Hawkes)** for tempo — each incursion raises short-run intensity with a decay parameter, which is the actual empirical signature of the series — **coupled to a repeated ladder game** in which PRC picks a rung, Taiwan/US pick a response, and both carry a commitment/reputation state that ratchets. Solved by backward induction over a finite ladder with seeded draws on private cost shocks. Byte-identical per seed.
- **What the judge panel is:** ACH over the tempo bands plus the quarantine binary. Judges: **diagnosticity** (which observable — CCG hull-days, joint-drill naming conventions, reserve call-ups, notice-to-airmen patterns — actually separates *pre-position-for-quarantine* from *normalize*); **key-assumptions** (assumes the ratchet is real; falsifier = an observed sustained tempo *decrease* without a concession); **devil's advocate** (argues the series is a stationary count process and the game layer adds nothing — this judge is the strongest one in the panel and must be argued down with data, not rhetoric); **premortem**; **red team** with standing to force a rerun on a new rung type. Rubric axes: indicator diagnosticity, distributional calibration, ratchet-assumption support, novel-rung reasoning quality, provenance.
- **The config pack:** `actors/prc-taiwan-coercion.yaml` (rung ladder definition with observable signatures per rung, PRC/Taiwan/US payoff parameters, calendar constraints); `kernel/hawkes-ladder.json` (branching ratio, decay, ladder transition costs); `rubric/coercion-ach.yaml`; `adapter/adiz-domain.ts` (bands defined directly in CSIS ChinaPower / Taiwan MND counting conventions); `thresholds/abstain.yaml` (abstain on the quarantine binary if no rung-specific indicator has been observed — the honest answer for a zero-base-rate event is often INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE, and the platform can prove its abstention gate fires).
- **Data required:** **Unclassified:** CSIS ChinaPower Taiwan ADIZ violations dataset (Brown & Lewis), Taiwan MND daily releases, CSIS Futures Lab maritime gray-zone work using AIS and fishing-effort data, commercial AIS, NOTAM/NAVAREA notices, PRC state-media framing. **Classified:** PLA readiness and intent reporting. **Unclassified demo: YES — and this is the single best demo in the catalog, because resolution data is public and daily.**
- **How accuracy is MEASURED:** **Live post-cutoff resolution, monthly** — the cleanest evidence-class (c) arm available. Register 180-day band forecasts, score with Brier on bands and CRPS on the count distribution, against public MND/CSIS tallies. Plus a **historical backtest** with as-of cutoffs at Aug 2022 (Pelosi visit) and Apr 2023, against the CSIS-documented 2020–2025 escalation (daily-average distinct CCG vessels in Taiwan's near waters up >500% Jan 2020–Dec 2025; sorties above 300/month for eight straight months per Taiwanese reporting). Baselines that must be beaten: a negative-binomial with month effects, and a plain Hawkes fit with no game layer.
- **Benefit categories hit:** **(b)** turns a narrative watch product into a scored distribution with a monthly resolution cycle; **(a)** the rung-signature table tells a collector what to look for; **(c)** reconciles Taiwan MND counts, Japanese MoD scramble data and commercial AIS where they disagree on the same day; **(d)** the daily tally-and-narrative task is automated.
- **Prior art and incumbents:** *Government:* **DARPA COMPASS**, explicitly built to blend AI, modeling and game theory to read adversary intent in gray-zone activity by gauging responses to stimuli — the closest programmatic ancestor; **DIU/Scale AI "Thunderforge"** for AI-assisted operational planning and wargaming at INDOPACOM and EUCOM (reports that Palantir later took over the effort are **UNVERIFIED**). *Open research:* CSIS ChinaPower and Futures Lab. *Commercial:* Rhombus Power "Guardian"; the widely repeated claim that Guardian anticipated the Feb 2022 Russian offensive months in advance is a **vendor/press claim, independently UNVERIFIED**.
- **Failure modes and red lines:** *Technical:* the discrete never-observed rung has zero training rows — the model can only reason about it structurally, and an over-confident number there is the most likely way this embarrasses the customer, which is exactly what the abstention gate exists to prevent. Ratchet assumptions can lock the model into monotone escalation and miss genuine de-escalation. *Ethical/legal:* outputs feed operational planning; the published escalation literature is a real warning — Rivera et al., *Escalation Risks from Language Models in Military and Diplomatic Decision-Making*, found all five off-the-shelf LLMs studied showed escalation and hard-to-predict escalation patterns, with arms-race dynamics and rare simulated nuclear use. **Structural enforcement:** the LLM never chooses an action — it parameterizes and interprets; the ladder and payoffs are config authored by humans; and the product schema has no COA-recommendation field, so the system cannot emit "what we should do," only "what is likely and what would change it."

### C-GEO-4 — Ceasefire Survival and Clause-Level Failure Attribution

- **Pitch (1 sentence):** Reads a signed peace or ceasefire agreement, simulates the parties' incentives to cheat as their relative strength changes, and reports both the odds the deal survives a year and which specific clause breaks first.
- **The analyst question it answers:** "Will the [X] ceasefire signed on [date] survive 12 months with no calendar month exceeding 25 UCDP-coded battle deaths (bands: survives clean / survives with minor violations / collapses in months 1–3 / collapses in months 4–8 / collapses in months 9–12), and which agreement provision is violated first?"
- **Mission and user:** State's Bureau of Conflict and Stabilization Operations; AFRICOM and CENTCOM J5 plans; NIC; DIA regional analysis supporting negotiation teams. **Today:** an officer reads the agreement text, compares it by analogy to two or three remembered cases, and writes a durability paragraph. The clause-level implementation calendar lives in a Word file nobody scores.
- **What the N simulants are:** N = 30–50 runs. State variables per armed actor: force strength trajectory, external patron support, resource-rent access, internal cohesion (splinter risk), expected post-implementation political share, and verification exposure. Agreement-level state: DDR sequencing, power-sharing shares, third-party guarantor presence and capacity, amnesty scope. **riskTolerance** governs each actor's tolerance for accepting verification and disarmament ahead of guarantees; **escalationPropensity** governs whether a grievance surfaces as a protest/withdrawal or as a resumed offensive. Runs vary over mutually exclusive futures for the *power trajectory* (relative strength rising / flat / falling for the weaker signatory) and for guarantor credibility — the two things the commitment-problem literature says determine survival.
- **What the deterministic kernel computes:** A **competing-risks survival hazard** (Weibull or Cox with time-varying covariates, competing causes: signatory defection / splinter spoiler / external-patron-driven resumption), where the covariates are *generated* by a **commitment-problem bargaining model** — if a party's expected future power declines faster than a threshold, its present incentive to defect rises, per the Fearon/Powell formalization. Spoilers arrive as a seeded Poisson process of factions with no seat at the table. Deterministic given seed.
- **What the judge panel is:** ACH over the five survival bands. Judges: **diagnosticity** on implementation milestones (which missed milestone actually predicts collapse versus which are routinely missed in surviving deals); **key-assumptions** (the power-trajectory assumption with an explicit falsifier tied to observable force-posture indicators); **devil's advocate** arguing the strong empirical base rate that most ceasefires do fail, so a "collapse" call carries little information and the burden is on "survives"; **premortem**; **red team** on whether the clause extraction from agreement text was faithful. Rubric axes: milestone diagnosticity, base-rate discipline, text-to-feature fidelity, spoiler treatment, provenance.
- **The config pack:** `actors/<conflict>-signatories.yaml`; `kernel/ceasefire-hazard.json` (baseline hazard shape, commitment-threshold parameter, spoiler arrival rate); `rubric/ceasefire-ach.yaml`; `adapter/pax-uc dp-domain.ts` (maps agreement text to the PA-X coding scheme and defines the failure event in UCDP GED terms so resolution is mechanical); `thresholds/abstain.yaml` (abstain when guarantor capacity is unobservable).
- **Data required:** **Unclassified:** PA-X Peace Agreements Database (University of Edinburgh; 1990–present, ~225 coded categories, current through Dec 2025 per the site), PA-X Local Agreements dataset, UCDP GED, ACLED, UN peacekeeping mandates and SG reports. **Classified:** patron intent and covert resupply. **Unclassified demo: YES.**
- **How accuracy is MEASURED:** **Historical backtest with as-of cutoff at signature date**, over PA-X agreements 1990–2020 — named episodes: Colombia–FARC 2016 (held) versus repeated ELN processes (failed), South Sudan ARCSS 2015 (failed within a year) versus R-ARCSS 2018 (partially held), Ethiopia–TPLF Pretoria Nov 2022, Libya Oct 2020, successive DRC/M23 processes. Metric: Brier skill against a **PA-X-coded logistic/hazard baseline**, which in this domain is genuinely strong. Plus **live post-cutoff** on currently active agreements.
- **Benefit categories hit:** **(c)** the clause-level ledger reconciles implementation reporting from UN, mediator and party sources and reports the contradictions rather than smoothing them; **(b)** produces the durability assessment plus a monitored milestone list in hours; **(d)** the recurring "is implementation on track" product is automated.
- **Prior art and incumbents:** *Government/academic:* PA-X (PeaceRep, Edinburgh); UCDP; the ceasefire-survival literature (Clayton & Sticher on ceasefire types and odds of survival). *Commercial:* none squarely; conflict-risk subscriptions (Verisk Maplecroft, Control Risks) address country risk, not clause-level durability (**UNVERIFIED** product detail).
- **Failure modes and red lines:** *Technical, and stated bluntly:* **this is the domain where the statistical baseline is hardest to beat.** Survival analysis on PA-X features is mature and well-tuned. The white paper should not claim the headline probability beats it; the defensible claim is **clause-level failure attribution with a falsifier per clause**, which no hazard model produces, and the joint delivery of both from one run. If clause attribution cannot be scored against observed first-violation coding, this candidate's distinctive claim degrades toward unscorable and it should be cut. *Ethical/legal:* assessments about live negotiations could be read as pressure on a party. **Structural enforcement:** no negotiating-recommendation output type; the product carries only likelihood, confidence, assumptions and indicators.
- **TRL now / TRL in 12 months:** **3 / 5** — lower than its neighbors because the text-to-clause extraction seam is the least built part.

### C-GEO-6 — Water-and-Food Stress to Violence: Pathway Attribution

- **Pitch (1 sentence):** For each district in a drought-stressed region, forecasts not just *whether* violence rises but *through which mechanism* — herder-farmer competition, urban price riot, or insurgent recruitment — so the response can match the cause.
- **The analyst question it answers:** "Which admin-1 units in the Sahel and Horn will record ≥1 organized-violence event per month for three consecutive months within the next nine months (bands over the count of qualifying units per country), and for each, which of three pathways dominates?"
- **Mission and user:** AFRICOM J2 and CENTCOM J2; DIA Africa/Middle East analysis; DHS I&A for onward-migration implications; State's political-military bureaus. **Today:** an analyst reads FEWS NET and IPC products in one window and ACLED in another, and asserts a causal link in prose with no test attached.
- **What the N simulants are:** N = 40–60 runs over admin-1 units. State variables: rainfall/soil-moisture anomaly, pasture biomass, staple price index, herd size and transhumance route pressure, household coping stock, displacement outflow, armed-group recruitment wage differential, and grievance stock. **riskTolerance** governs the household coping threshold at which migration or armed-group joining is chosen over asset sale; **escalationPropensity** governs whether local grievance surfaces as communal violence or as insurgent-aligned violence. Runs vary over mutually exclusive climate futures (drought persists / breaks / shifts spatially) crossed with governance response (subsidy / nothing / coercive requisition).
- **What the deterministic kernel computes:** A **coupled system-dynamics model** — rainfall anomaly → yield and pasture → local price → household coping choice → grievance stock — with explicit lag structure, plus a **spatial diffusion term** across adjacent admin units (displacement and herd movement carry stress downstream), and a **discrete pathway state machine** so the output names a mechanism rather than emitting a scalar risk score. Deterministic given seed.
- **What the judge panel is:** ACH over the three pathways plus a null. Judges: **diagnosticity** (which observable separates herder-farmer from insurgent-recruitment violence — victim/perpetrator coding in ACLED, seasonality, event geography relative to transhumance corridors); **key-assumptions** (the price-to-grievance elasticity, falsified by an observed price spike with no violence response); **devil's advocate** — here the strongest judge, arguing the pathway label is unfalsifiable post-hoc storytelling; **premortem**; **red team**. Rubric axes: pathway falsifiability, elasticity grounding, spatial-lag justification, ACLED-coding fidelity, provenance.
- **The config pack:** `actors/<region>-livelihood-strata.yaml`; `kernel/hydro-price-grievance.json` (yield response curves, price pass-through, lag lengths, diffusion coefficients); `rubric/resource-pathway-ach.yaml`; `adapter/acled-fewsnet-domain.ts` (pathway labels defined in terms of ACLED actor/event-type combinations so the pathway claim is *scorable*, which is the whole viability question for this candidate); `thresholds/abstain.yaml`.
- **Data required:** **Unclassified:** FEWS NET, IPC, the Water Peace and Security Global Early Warning Tool (WRI-hosted; 12-month ML conflict forecasts for Africa, the Middle East and South/Southeast Asia), ACLED, FAO GIEWS price series, CHIRPS rainfall, GRACE groundwater, WorldPop. **Classified:** minimal — this is among the most unclassified-native candidates. **Unclassified demo: YES.**
- **How accuracy is MEASURED:** **Historical backtest with as-of cutoff** on 2015–2023 Sahel/Horn district-months, plus **live post-cutoff** on rolling nine-month windows. Two separate scores: (i) the count-band forecast, scored with Brier skill against **ACLED CAST and the WPS Global Early Warning Tool as named incumbent baselines** — WPS publicly claims capture of 86% of future conflicts, a recall figure with no stated precision, so the comparison must be made on a common metric, not on their number; (ii) the pathway label, scored against ACLED actor-type coding of the realized events (precision/recall per pathway).
- **Benefit categories hit:** **(c)** reconciles FEWS NET/IPC food-security phase classifications against ACLED violence coding, which today disagree constantly and are never formally compared; **(b)** turns a two-source manual read into a scored product; **(a)** pathway attribution changes the response, which is what accuracy means here; **(d)** the quarterly regional refresh is automated.
- **Prior art and incumbents:** *Government/NGO:* FEWS NET (USAID-lineage, six-to-twelve-month food-insecurity outlooks), IPC, WPS Global Early Warning Tool (WRI/IHE Delft/Deltares consortium), ACLED CAST. *Commercial:* Fraym for subnational population analytics (**UNVERIFIED** on forecasting).
- **Failure modes and red lines:** **This is the candidate most at risk of losing to the incumbent, and the white paper must say so.** WPS and ACLED CAST already produce district-level twelve-month forecasts from ML on essentially these features and are well tuned. The headline count-band forecast will very likely *not* beat them. The only defensible added value is **pathway attribution with a per-pathway falsifier** plus the **policy-conditional** branch ("if the borehole/subsidy program runs, what changes"), which observational ML cannot answer. If pathway labels cannot be scored against ACLED coding at usable precision, the distinctive claim collapses and the candidate should be dropped rather than defended. *Ethical/legal:* low sensitivity; the main risk is that resource-stress forecasts get used to justify pre-emptive coercive posture. **Structural enforcement:** no action-recommendation output type; geography resolution floored at admin-1 so no settlement-level targeting product is representable.
- **TRL now / TRL in 12 months:** **4 / 6** — conditional on the pathway-scoring seam working; if it does not, TRL is irrelevant because the candidate is dead.

### C-GEO-7 — Collapse Consequence Fan (conditional second-order effects)

- **Pitch (1 sentence):** Answers the planner's question that comes *after* the instability question — if central authority in this country fails, how many people cross which borders in the first ninety days, and where does the reception system break.
- **The analyst question it answers:** "Conditional on an irregular loss of central authority in [country] within 12 months, what is the distribution of cross-border displacement in the first 90 days, by receiving country, in bands (<50k / 50–250k / 250k–1M / >1M), and which crossing point saturates first?"
- **Mission and user:** DHS Office of Intelligence and Analysis; State Bureau of Population, Refugees and Migration; EUCOM and AFRICOM J5 planners; ODNI/NIC. **Today:** a planner takes a rule-of-thumb multiple of a prior crisis ("assume Syria-scale, discounted") and a country expert argues about the multiplier in a meeting. There is no route model, no capacity model, and no uncertainty band.
- **What the N simulants are:** N = 50–80 runs. Agent populations by origin admin unit with state variables: displacement propensity (a function of local violence exposure and asset stock), destination preference (diaspora pull, language, prior migration corridor), route cost including checkpoint and smuggling prices, and border-gate state (open / quota'd / closed). Receiving-side state: reception capacity, processing rate, onward-movement rate. **riskTolerance** governs how much route danger a household accepts; **escalationPropensity** governs whether receiving states harden borders in response to arrivals. Runs vary over mutually exclusive collapse geographies (capital-centric / periphery-first / fragmentation into two zones) crossed with border-policy responses.
- **What the deterministic kernel computes:** An **agent-based displacement model with a gravity/discrete-choice route-selection layer** over the real road network, plus **queueing** at crossing points and reception centers (arrival process vs. service rate → saturation time), plus a **network cascade** for onward secondary movement when a receiving node saturates. Seeded PRNG for household draws; deterministic given seed.
- **What the judge panel is:** ACH over the receiving-country band vectors. Judges: **diagnosticity** (which early observable — border-town price spikes, fuel queues, first-mover flows — separates the collapse geographies); **key-assumptions** (diaspora-pull coefficients, falsified by observed early flows going the "wrong" direction); **devil's advocate** (argues historical analogy beats the ABM and the prior-crisis multiple is a strong baseline — it usually is); **premortem**; **red team** on whether route capacity was measured or assumed. Rubric axes: route-data grounding, capacity realism, analogy-baseline comparison, conditional framing discipline, provenance.
- **The config pack:** `actors/<country>-population-strata.yaml`; `kernel/displacement-abm.json` (propensity curves, route costs, gate policies, service rates); `rubric/displacement-ach.yaml`; `adapter/unhcr-iom-domain.ts` (bands defined in UNHCR/IOM counting conventions so scoring is mechanical); `thresholds/abstain.yaml` (abstain when road-network or crossing-capacity data is stale).
- **Data required:** **Unclassified:** UNHCR Operational Data Portal, IOM Displacement Tracking Matrix, IDMC GRID, WorldPop, OpenStreetMap road network, historical crossing-point throughput, WFP market price data. **Classified:** border-force intent and any covert facilitation. **Unclassified demo: YES.**
- **How accuracy is MEASURED:** **Historical backtest conditioned on the trigger having occurred** — Syria 2012–2016, Venezuela 2015–2019, Ukraine Feb 2022, Afghanistan Aug 2021, Sudan Apr 2023, Syria Dec 2024. Score the 90-day displacement distribution with CRPS against UNHCR/IOM counts. **This measures the consequence model, not the trigger model**, and the two must never be pooled — the platform's mandatory evaluation-regime tag is doing real work here. Baseline to beat: the prior-crisis analogy multiple that planners actually use, plus a simple gravity model with no capacity constraint. **Honest limitation:** conditional forecasts are only scorable on branches that realized, so the sample is small and selection-biased toward large crises; this must be stated in the product, not hidden.
- **Benefit categories hit:** **(b)** produces in hours a planning number that today takes a week of meetings, with a band instead of a point; **(d)** the same config reruns across a dozen watch countries; **(a)** the saturating-crossing-point output is directly actionable for posture and pre-positioning; **(c)** reconciles UNHCR, IOM DTM and host-government counts, which routinely disagree by large factors, and reports the disagreement.
- **Prior art and incumbents:** *Government/IO:* UNHCR and IOM operational forecasting; the EU's Knowledge Centre on Migration and Demography and the migration-forecasting literature surveyed in the JRC/arXiv "Data Innovation in Demography, Migration and Human Mobility" work. *Commercial:* none squarely; Palantir's Foundry-based humanitarian deployments are data integration rather than displacement simulation (**UNVERIFIED**).
- **Failure modes and red lines:** *Technical:* ABMs of migration are extremely sensitive to unmeasured route capacity and to smuggling-market response, and they can produce precise-looking numbers with no support — the abstention gate on stale route data is the main defense. *Ethical/legal:* **this is the candidate with the sharpest red line.** Displacement modeling that touches US borders sits directly on US-persons and immigration-enforcement equities. **Structural enforcement:** the output schema has no person-level or household-level field — the smallest representable unit is an admin-1 aggregate flow — so a person-level or family-level product is unrepresentable; any run whose receiving country resolves to the United States is additionally forced to admin-1-and-above aggregation by the domain adapter, and there is no code path from an aggregate flow object to an identity or a location fix.
- **TRL now / TRL in 12 months:** **3 / 5.**

### C-GEO-8 — The Contradiction Ledger (multi-source reconciliation and analytic-confidence QA)

- **Pitch (1 sentence):** Reads a whole issue folder and reports exactly where the sources contradict each other on the same checkable claim, and which of those contradictions, if resolved, would actually change the bottom line.
- **The analyst question it answers:** "Across the 340 reports underlying this assessment, on which specific resolvable claims do our sources disagree, and for each disagreement, does resolving it in either direction move the BLUF likelihood band?" The resolvable quantity is the count and location of band-flipping contradictions — measurable directly.
- **Mission and user:** Any Directorate of Analysis production office plus the tradecraft/QA cell that reviews drafts against ODNI analytic standards (ICD 203 — **URL UNVERIFIED this session**); ODNI's analytic-integrity function; the front office that has to defend a confidence level in a briefing. **Today:** a senior reviewer reads the draft and the source list and catches contradictions from memory, at a rate that does not scale past a few dozen sources, and the "confidence" line is set by negotiation rather than by anything computed.
- **What the N simulants are:** N is not scenario-simulation here but **evidence-partition resampling** — each run instantiates a different admissible partition of the source base (by stream, by originating agency, by access, by date window) and a different draw of source-reliability parameters from their track-record posteriors. State variables: per-claim posterior, per-source reliability, per-claim diagnosticity weight, conflict mass. **riskTolerance** sets how much source-reliability uncertainty is tolerated before a contradiction is escalated; **escalationPropensity** sets whether a detected inconsistency is reported as a note or forces a rerun. *This is genuinely different variation from the other candidates and should be described as such rather than dressed up as scenario simulation.*
- **What the deterministic kernel computes:** **Bayesian belief propagation over an evidence network**, with contradiction quantified as **Dempster–Shafer conflict mass K** (or Jensen–Shannon divergence) between the posteriors implied by disjoint source partitions, and **Heuer ACH diagnosticity weighting** deciding which contradictions matter — specifically, a sensitivity computation identifying which contradictions, when resolved either way, flip a likelihood band. Deterministic given seed; no wall clock. **This is a real kernel, not a wrapper: the math is belief update plus band-flip sensitivity.**
- **What the judge panel is:** ACH over the competing readings of the evidence base; key-assumptions check on every unsourced assertion; devil's advocate arguing the contradictions are artifacts of coding rather than substance; premortem on "the contradiction we flagged was noise and the one we missed was the war"; red team with standing to force a bounded rerun on a new source partition. Rubric axes: contradiction precision, contradiction recall, band-flip relevance, provenance completeness, unsupported-assertion rate, and confidence-vs-likelihood separation (a draft that collapses the two is flagged, since the platform's own type system forbids it).
- **The config pack:** `adapter/<domain>-claim-schema.yaml` (what counts as the same resolvable claim in this domain — the hardest and most domain-specific file); `kernel/evidence-network.json` (prior reliabilities, conflict measure, band-flip thresholds); `rubric/qa-tradecraft.yaml` (ICD 203-aligned axes); `sources/reliability-priors.yaml` (per-stream track record); `thresholds/abstain.yaml`.
- **Data required:** **Unclassified:** any document corpus — open-source demo runs on news and think-tank corpora with injected contradictions. **Classified:** the real value is on all-source finished and raw reporting inside the enclave. **Unclassified demo: YES, and it is the most convincing demo for benefit category (c)** because contradiction precision/recall can be shown live on a corpus with known injected errors.
- **How accuracy is MEASURED:** **The best-scored candidate in the catalog.** (i) **Synthetic oracle:** corpora with contradictions injected at known type, location and severity → contradiction precision and recall measured directly, plus false-abstention rate. This is exactly the regime the prototype has already exercised at 15,832 simulations. (ii) **Historical:** estimative products whose source base was later publicly recanted — the Iraq WMD aluminum-tubes dispute and the CURVEBALL reporting are the canonical open cases — scored on whether the system flags the contradiction the post-mortems identified. (iii) **SME panel** on whether flagged contradictions were the ones that mattered. Not unscorable in any arm.
- **Benefit categories hit:** **(c)** is the entire product — identify discrepancies between sources, reconcile them, and attach a defensible confidence; **(a)** review coverage goes from a reviewer's memory to exhaustive over the source base; **(d)** the pre-publication tradecraft check is the repetitive task being automated; **(b)** review turnaround drops.
- **Prior art and incumbents:** *Government:* **IARPA REASON** is the direct ancestor — a 42-month program beginning 1 Oct 2023 (Phase 1 through 30 Sep 2025, Phase 2 option through 31 Mar 2027), in partnership with DEVCOM ARL, explicitly to point analysts at key evidence and help determine which alternative explanations have the strongest support, automatically as the analyst writes. **The white paper must acknowledge this overlap head-on rather than claim novelty.** Differentiation is on (1) measured abstention as a first-class output, (2) the calibration layer that removes judge severity bias by z-score normalization and aggregates confidence by weakest link, and (3) the structural refusal to collapse likelihood and confidence into one number. *Commercial:* Primer, Palantir Foundry's analytic-provenance tooling (**UNVERIFIED** as contradiction detection).
- **Failure modes and red lines:** *Technical:* claim-identity resolution — deciding two reports are about the *same* claim — is the whole ballgame and is where this fails silently; a domain adapter with a sloppy claim schema will report contradictions that are paraphrase artifacts, destroying trust on first contact. Contradiction precision must therefore be reported before recall in every product. *Ethical/legal:* a system that scores reporting streams is one config change away from scoring individual analysts. **Structural enforcement:** the metrics schema aggregates only by source *stream* and by *claim*, and contains no analyst-identity field — there is structurally no function from a finished product to a person — and this is the same design move the platform already makes in refusing any function of type `(Judgment) => number`.
- **TRL now / TRL in 12 months:** **5 / 7** — the highest in the catalog, because the existing prototype's ACH, provenance, calibration and abstention machinery is most of this candidate already.

### C-GEO-9 — Post-Election Legitimacy Crisis

- **Pitch (1 sentence):** For a scheduled election on a known date, forecasts which of five political end-states follows — clean transfer, contested-then-conceded, sustained unrest with the incumbent holding, incumbent removed, or annulment — and does it early enough to matter.
- **The analyst question it answers:** "For [country]'s election on [date], what is the probability of each of five mutually exclusive states at day 120: (1) result accepted, transfer or continuation without major protest; (2) contested then conceded via courts or negotiation; (3) contested with sustained unrest, incumbent in office; (4) contested, incumbent out of office; (5) election annulled, postponed or re-run?" Bands are exhaustive and mutually exclusive by construction, with ACLED counts and office-holding as the resolution quantities.
- **Mission and user:** State INR's election watch; NIC; CIA regional analysis; embassy country teams building contingency plans. **Today:** the country team writes a pre-election cable with three narrative scenarios and no probabilities, and the post-election assessment is written after the fact.
- **What the N simulants are:** N = 40–70 runs. Actors: incumbent, principal opposition, security services, courts/electoral commission, external guarantors. State variables: incumbent's private estimate of losing, manipulation capacity and cost, opposition mobilization capacity, security-service cohesion and pay arrears, judicial independence, observer-mission presence, and the street's outside option. **riskTolerance** governs incumbent willingness to manipulate under observation risk; **escalationPropensity** governs whether contestation is legal (courts) or street-based. Runs vary over mutually exclusive incumbent strategies (accept / manipulate at the margin / manipulate grossly / pre-emptively postpone) crossed with two security-service loyalty states.
- **What the deterministic kernel computes:** **Two stages, both deterministic given seed.** Stage 1: a **discrete-choice manipulation decision** — the incumbent trades expected cost of detected fraud (sanctions, defection, unrest) against probability of losing, yielding a manipulation intensity. Stage 2: a **three-player extensive-form bargaining game** (incumbent, opposition, security services) with an outside option for the street, solved by **backward induction to subgame-perfect equilibrium**, producing a distribution over the five end-states. Private-information draws come from the seeded PRNG.
- **What the judge panel is:** ACH over the five states. Judges: **diagnosticity** (which pre-election observable — turnout-forensic anomalies, opposition unification, security-service pay arrears, observer-mission access denial — separates state 2 from state 3); **key-assumptions** (security-service loyalty, falsified by observed non-payment or unit reshuffle); **devil's advocate** arguing state 1 from the base rate, since most elections resolve quietly; **premortem**; **red team** on whether turnout forensics were used as evidence when they are weak evidence. Rubric axes: pre-election indicator diagnosticity, base-rate discipline, forensic-evidence discipline, falsifier quality, provenance.
- **The config pack:** `actors/<country>-election-actors.yaml`; `kernel/election-bargaining.json` (manipulation cost curves, payoff matrix, outside-option value); `rubric/election-ach.yaml`; `adapter/nelda-acled-domain.ts` (five states defined in NELDA + ACLED + office-holding terms); `thresholds/abstain.yaml` (abstain where opposition mobilization capacity is unobservable — the usual case in fully closed states).
- **Data required:** **Unclassified:** NELDA, V-Dem, ACLED, IFES election calendar, national electoral commission results, observer reports (EU EOM, OSCE/ODIHR, Carter Center), IODA/NetBlocks shutdown data, security-force pay reporting. **Classified:** incumbent's private intent and security-service reporting. **Unclassified demo: YES.**
- **How accuracy is MEASURED:** **Historical backtest with as-of cutoff at 30 days pre-poll** — Belarus Aug 2020 (state 3), Kenya Aug 2022 (state 2, resolved in court), Zimbabwe Aug 2023 (state 3), Bangladesh Jan 2024 (state 3) and the separate Jul–Aug 2024 ouster, Venezuela Jul 2024 (state 3), Georgia Oct 2024, Mozambique Oct 2024, Brazil Oct 2022 (state 2). **Live post-cutoff: the strongest sustained arm in the catalog after C-GEO-3** — the national election calendar supplies a steady stream of date-certain, pre-registrable, publicly resolvable five-way questions, which is exactly what a calibration curve needs and what most intelligence questions never provide. Baselines: the historical distribution over the five states conditioned on regime type, and an election-violence logistic on V-Dem + ACLED covariates.
- **Benefit categories hit:** **(b)** date-certain questions mean the forecast arrives before the event and can be scored after, closing the loop that current cable-writing never closes; **(a)** the five-state joint output is what contingency planning actually needs, versus a violence count; **(c)** reconciles observer-mission findings, electoral-commission returns and opposition parallel tabulations, which routinely conflict; **(d)** the pre-election assessment template runs across the whole annual calendar.
- **Prior art and incumbents:** *Government/academic:* NELDA and the election-violence forecasting literature; PITF; ACLED's election-violence tracking. *Commercial:* Verisk Maplecroft and Control Risks election-risk products, which are narrative and country-level (**UNVERIFIED** on probabilistic scoring). Whether a USAID-successor democracy-programming office remains a viable user in 2026 is **UNVERIFIED** — the DoD/IC users listed do not depend on it.
- **Failure modes and red lines:** *Technical:* turnout forensics (digit tests and similar) are weak, widely misused evidence and will tempt the model into confident wrong calls — hence a dedicated rubric axis. Backward induction over a hand-specified extensive form is only as good as the tree, and the tree is config, so a bad config produces a confident bad answer with full provenance, which is the most dangerous failure shape this platform has. *Ethical/legal:* forecasting election outcomes in foreign states is adjacent to election interference. **Structural enforcement:** no influence, messaging or resource-allocation output type exists in the schema; the domain adapter refuses to load any US or US-territory electoral geography, so a US-election product cannot be produced even by misconfiguration; the corpus retrieval layer applies US-persons filtering before the kernel runs.
- **TRL now / TRL in 12 months:** **4 / 6.**

### C-GEO-10 — **BAD IDEA (included as the catalog floor):** The Global Instability Heat Map

- **Pitch (1 sentence):** A monthly 0–100 instability score for every country on earth, rendered as a colored world map with a drill-down page per country. **This is a bad candidate and is included so the catalog has a floor.**
- **The analyst question it answers:** **There isn't one.** "How unstable is Country X, 0 to 100?" is not resolvable against any quantity. Every attempt to make it resolvable collapses it into one of the nine candidates above (leader exit, protest counts, fatalities, displacement), at which point the index itself is doing no work. This is the disqualifying defect and it is not fixable by better engineering.
- **Mission and user:** Nominally "senior policymakers." In practice nobody — the failure signature of this product class is that it is briefed once at rollout, printed for a wall, and then ignored, because it never tells anyone to do anything differently. **Today** those users read PITF-lineage products, ViEWS, ACLED CAST and commercial country-risk subscriptions that already do exactly this, better and cheaper.
- **What the N simulants are:** Nothing meaningful varies. The same covariate vector is fed through the same weighting N times; the run-to-run variance is sampling noise dressed as ensemble diversity. **This candidate fails the variation test in the brief outright and I am flagging it rather than hiding it.** riskTolerance and escalationPropensity have no defined referent at the level of "a country's instability," because there is no actor making a decision.
- **What the deterministic kernel computes:** **NO KERNEL.** There is no dynamic to simulate. A weighted sum of covariates is a regression, and the published incumbents already ship that regression with public baselines — the ViEWS leaderboard lists named benchmark models (`VIEWS_bm_ph_conflictology_country12`, `VIEWS_bm_last_historical`, `VIEWS_bm_conflictology_bootstrap240`, `VIEWS_bm_exactly_zero`) that any challenger must beat on CRPS, MIS and log score. A simulation ensemble with no dynamics will not beat them, and running N LLM-orchestrated simulations to reproduce a logistic regression is the worst cost-per-insight profile available.
- **What the judge panel is:** A panel can be assembled — ACH, key assumptions, devil's advocate — but it has nothing to adjudicate between, because the hypotheses are score bands over an unresolvable construct rather than outcomes over a resolvable quantity. The ACH matrix degenerates: every piece of evidence is weakly consistent with every band, diagnosticity goes to zero, and the panel's output is noise with a confidence label on it. That degeneracy is itself a useful demonstration and is worth one paragraph in the white paper.
- **The config pack:** `actors/none.yaml` (there are no actors), `kernel/weighted-index.json`, `rubric/generic.yaml`, `adapter/country-index-domain.ts`, `thresholds/none.yaml`. The thinness of this pack relative to the other nine is the tell.
- **Data required:** All unclassified and all already public — World Bank WDI, V-Dem, ACLED, POLECAT, Fragile States Index inputs. **An unclassified demo is trivially possible, which is precisely the trap:** it demos beautifully and is worthless, and a customer who has funded PITF since the 1990s will recognize it in thirty seconds.
- **How accuracy is MEASURED:** **UNSCORABLE as specified** — no oracle exists for a 0–100 construct. Forcing an oracle means picking a proxy outcome, at which point the honest move is to forecast that outcome directly, which is candidates 1 through 9. Marked disqualifying.
- **Benefit categories hit:** **None defensibly.** (a) is unclaimable because accuracy is undefined; (b) is unclaimable because the incumbent is faster; (c) is not addressed at all — an index averages sources rather than reconciling them, which is the opposite of what the customer asked for; (d) is negative, since N-run simulation across ~190 countries monthly costs far more than the regression it imitates.
- **Prior art and incumbents:** **Saturated.** *Government:* PITF; **ViEWS** (Uppsala/PRIO), which runs a public prediction challenge with 13 participating institutions, monthly-updated live evaluation, and published baselines; **ACLED CAST**. *Commercial:* Verisk Maplecroft, Economist Intelligence Unit and RANE country-risk indices (**UNVERIFIED** product detail). Competing here is competing on brand against thirty years of incumbency with a worse instrument.
- **Failure modes and red lines:** *Technical:* an always-on map with no falsifier trains analysts to ignore it; the one month it turns red for a real reason is indistinguishable from the eleven months it was red for noise, which is the classic early-warning crying-wolf failure the customer has already lived through. *Ethical/legal:* low direct risk, but there is a real institutional harm — shipping an unscorable index alongside nine scorable products contaminates the credibility of all ten, because the customer will reasonably ask why one of them has no oracle.
- **TRL now / TRL in 12 months:** **6 / 6.** It is buildable today and would still be worthless in a year, which is the point: **buildability is not the constraint in this market; scorability is.**

## Baseline-beating test, applied honestly to all ten

The brief's rule — a candidate that cannot beat a logistic regression on ACLED data does not belong in a white paper — separates the catalog cleanly:

- **Beats the statistical baseline on the headline quantity:** none, reliably, and the white paper should not claim otherwise. On well-covered outcomes (country-month fatalities, coup-years, district violence counts) a well-tuned ML baseline — ViEWS challenge entrants, ACLED CAST, WPS — will match or beat an LLM-orchestrated ensemble. Current evidence points the same way on general forecasting: ForecastBench found superforecasters at Brier 0.068, the general public at 0.083, and the best LLM at 0.119, though a recent agentic system (AIA Forecaster) reports superforecaster-level performance on that benchmark — a technical report, not an independent replication.
- **Beats it on things a regression structurally cannot do**, which is where all nine viable candidates should be pitched: (1) **policy-conditional counterfactuals** — C-GEO-2 (internet shutdown), C-GEO-3 (US declaratory move), C-GEO-5 (new designation), C-GEO-6 (borehole program); (2) **zero-base-rate discrete events** with no training rows — C-GEO-3's quarantine rung; (3) **decomposition and attribution with falsifiers** — C-GEO-1's per-actor sensitivity, C-GEO-4's clause attribution, C-GEO-6's pathway labels; (4) **multi-source contradiction reconciliation** — C-GEO-8, orthogonal to any base-rate model and the direct hit on the customer's benefit category (c); (5) **measured abstention**, which no regression offers — a logistic always emits a number.
- **The framing that survives contact with a PITF-literate customer:** this platform is not a replacement for the statistical early-warning stack, it is the layer that is *ensembled with and audited by* it — and it is the only layer that reports its own false-abstention rate, keeps likelihood and confidence structurally separate, and refuses to pool synthetic, backtest and live evidence regimes.
- **The one methodological threat that applies to every backtest above:** parametric memorization. As-of corpus retrieval with a hard date filter and a runtime assertion on post-dated evidence stops *retrieval* leakage; it does not stop a model that already knows Niger 2023 happened. Every historical-backtest number in this catalog is an upper bound, and only the live post-cutoff arms — C-GEO-3 (monthly, public) and C-GEO-9 (date-certain election calendar) — are evidence-class (c). Saying this in the white paper is a differentiator, not a weakness.
