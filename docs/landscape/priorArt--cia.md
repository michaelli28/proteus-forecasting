## 1. Who inside CIA would own this

### 1.1 Directorate of Analysis (DA) — the mission owner

**Mission (verbatim, [cia.gov/about/organization/directorate-of-analysis/](https://www.cia.gov/about/organization/directorate-of-analysis/), fetched 10 Aug 2026):** "The Directorate of Analysis (DA) provides timely and objective intelligence analysis that informs U.S. officials on key national and foreign policy issues. DA officers use their expertise, creativity, digital acumen, teamwork, and critical thinking to decipher and synthesize incomplete — and sometimes contradictory — information to make sense of a complex world." `CONFIRMED`

**Products.** Flagship is the **CIA World Intelligence Review (WIRe)**, a daily electronic publication for senior US foreign/security policy officials, the military and Congress. DA also contributes to the **President's Daily Brief (PDB)**, an IC product overseen by ODNI. DA officers also write memos responding to specific questions or supporting policy meetings. `CONFIRMED` — same page.

**Its three stated priorities** — and the middle one is the door:
- *Tradecraft & Expertise* — "timely, relevant, and insightful analysis that is grounded in deep substantive expertise and tradecraft"
- ***Analytic Innovation* — "The DA leverages emerging technology, develops new analytic approaches, and aligns CIA technical capabilities to make sense of an increasingly complex and digitally connected world."**
- *Organizational Resilience*
`CONFIRMED` — same page.

**History/self-conception.** Established 1952; roots in the OSS Research & Analysis Branch. CIA's own account of DA lineage says OSS veteran **Sherman Kent** "championed intellectual rigor, conscious efforts to avoid bias, collective responsibility of judgment, precision of language, systematic use of outside experts to preempt analytic blind spots, and the need for close ties to the policy world… This remains the DA's ethos nearly eight decades later." `CONFIRMED` — [Insights with Impact: The Directorate of Analysis](https://www.cia.gov/stories/story/insights-with-impact-the-directorate-of-analysis/).

**Leadership.**
- **Linda Weissgold** was Deputy Director for Analysis (DDA) March 2020 – April 2023; in that role "responsible for the quality of all-source intelligence analysis at the CIA and for the professional development of the officers who produce it." `CONFIRMED` — [Bush School bio](https://bush.tamu.edu/faculty/linda-weissgold-2/), [INSA](https://www.insaonline.org/detail-pages/person/linda-weissgold).
- **Current DDA: `UNVERIFIED`.** No public naming found.
- **Wayne McCool**, Associate Deputy Director of CIA for Analysis, listed Jan 2021 – Oct 2025 on his LinkedIn profile. `REPORTED` (self-published profile).

**The single most useful public artifact for this pitch** is McCool's February 2024 LinkedIn post laying out the DA's own AI strategy. Quoted from the profile record: the DA strategy "focuses on three pillars: **AI as an intelligence topic; AI as a mission enabler; and AI governance and evaluation.**" And, critically: *"We are horizontally scanning for new AI technology and novel use cases, **such as simulating crisis scenarios**."* Also: *"We are also ensuring AI-powered analysis meets our high standards of tradecraft… mitigating the risks it presents for us, such as **biases and lack of explainability of results**."* `REPORTED` — [LinkedIn post, 25 Feb 2024, via profile record](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/wayne-mccool-a1b5b0211_da-stepping-boldly-into-ai-its-been-a-few-activity-7167649335709523968-jbQr). Evidence class: a named senior DA officer's public post, not an official CIA release. Verify before quoting in a submission.

`INFERRED`: "simulating crisis scenarios" is the DA leadership's own words for the product category. A white paper that opens by quoting the buyer's own stated novel use case, and then supplies the missing third pillar (*governance and evaluation* — i.e. measured calibration, measured abstention, measured leakage control), is aimed at the exact seam the DA has already publicly said it has.

**Regional and functional Mission Centers** — the eleven listed on cia.gov as of 10 Aug 2026: Africa; Americas and Counternarcotics; **Center for Cyber Intelligence**; China; Counterintelligence; Counterterrorism; East Asia & Pacific; Europe & Eurasia; Near East; South & Central Asia; Weapons & Counterproliferation. Mission Centers "bring together a full range of operational, analytic, support, technical, and digital capabilities." `CONFIRMED` — [cia.gov/about/organization/](https://www.cia.gov/about/organization/). CIA's own analogy: "think of our directorates as the branches of service and our mission centers as the combatant commands." `CONFIRMED` — [Ask Molly: CIA's Mission Centers](https://www.cia.gov/stories/story/ask-molly-cias-mission-centers/) (note: that page is stale — it still lists Transnational & Technology and Western Hemisphere).

`INFERRED` mapping to the solicitation's worked example (Iran vs US, water infrastructure hacking): that question sits across **Near East MC** (Iranian decision-making), **Center for Cyber Intelligence** (the cyber operation and the strategic cyber analysis mission), and **DA** (the all-source judgment and the product). The CCI elevation makes it a first-class, direct-report customer for exactly that scenario class — CCI is described as "the agency's mission manager for cyber, including strategic analysis and operations." `CONFIRMED` — [The Record](https://therecord.media/cia-director-elevated-agency-cyber-espionage-division).

### 1.2 The Red Cell and the structured-techniques canon — where your adjudication panel already has a home

CIA's alternative-analysis institution is the **DCI Red Cell**, formed within days of 11 September 2001 on George Tenet's instruction to Deputy Director for Intelligence Jami Miscik: *"Tell me things others don't and make [senior officials] feel uncomfortable."* It is "devoted to 'alternative analysis,' which includes techniques like 'what ifs,' Team A/Team B exercises, and premortem analysis." Petraeus in 2012 directed it to "take on our most difficult challenges" and "shock us." Robert Gates: "I never skipped past one of them." `CONFIRMED` — [Foreign Policy, "Inside the CIA Red Cell"](https://foreignpolicy.com/2015/10/30/inside-the-cia-red-cell-micah-zenko-red-team-intelligence/).

CIA's own published **Tradecraft Primer: Structured Analytic Techniques for Improving Intelligence Analysis** organizes techniques into *diagnostic*, *contrarian*, and *imaginative thinking*, and names, among others: **Analysis of Competing Hypotheses (ACH)**, **Key Assumptions Check**, **Devil's Advocacy**, **Team A/Team B**, **High-Impact/Low-Probability Analysis**, **"What If?" Analysis**, **Indicators and Signposts**. On ACH specifically: *"a good tool to use throughout a project to prevent premature closure and to highlight evidence that is most 'discriminating' in making an analytic argument."* `CONFIRMED` — [CIA CSI, Tradecraft Primer (Apr 2009)](https://www.cia.gov/resources/csi/static/Tradecraft-Primer-apr09.pdf).

`INFERRED`, and this is the strongest single positioning fact in this report: **your adjudication panel is not a novel methodology you have to sell. It is the CIA's own published primer, implemented in software, with the diagnosticity weighting made explicit and the outputs measured.** ACH diagnosticity-weighting, key-assumptions check, devil's advocate, premortem, and a red team with standing are, one for one, the Primer's contrarian and diagnostic sets plus the Red Cell's charter. The white paper should say this in the first page and cite the Primer by name.

There is also documented institutional history of *failed* attempts to institutionalize challenge mechanisms — Colby's 1973–75 devil's-advocate initiative died on "the practicality of institutionalizing challenge procedures" and fears that "the system's (non-monetary) costs might outweigh its benefits." `CONFIRMED` — [CSI, "Instituting Devil's Advocacy in IC Analysis after the Arab-Israeli War of October 1973"](https://www.cia.gov/resources/csi/static/610d592f509c5ad03f5a999827dd9bdb/Article-Instituting-Devils-Advocacy-in-IC-Analysis-after-October-1973-War.pdf). `INFERRED`: the cost-of-rigor objection is the historical failure mode. Your cost-per-insight metric answers a fifty-year-old internal argument, and saying so is worth a paragraph.

The 2005 WMD Commission's language is also directly usable: the Community "should institute a formal system for competitive — and even explicitly contrarian — analysis. Such groups must be licensed to be troublesome. Further, they must take contrarian positions, not just ones that take a harder line (a flaw with the Team B exercise of the 1970s)." `CONFIRMED` — quoted in the same CSI article. `INFERRED`: "licensed to be troublesome" is a precise description of a red team with standing to force a bounded rerun.

### 1.3 Sherman Kent School / analytic tradecraft training

- Created 1999, opened May 2000 in Reston, VA; the DA's component of CIA University. `CONFIRMED` — [CIA LinkedIn, 9 Dec 2025](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/central-intelligence-agency_on-this-day-in-1999-cias-sherman-kent-school-activity-7404240349990121473-r-Uv); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sherman_Kent_School_for_Intelligence_Analysis); [Washington Post, 4 May 2000](https://www.washingtonpost.com/archive/politics/2000/05/04/cia-goes-deep-into-analysis/834f4dbd-b02d-4d1f-8b7e-b3337bf8304d/).
- Runs the **Career Analyst Program (CAP)** — "teaches all new DA officers the foundational skills of critical thinking, writing, and briefing, as well as how to maintain objectivity and integrity throughout their analytic career." `CONFIRMED` — [CIA, Insights with Impact](https://www.cia.gov/stories/story/insights-with-impact-the-directorate-of-analysis/).
- Founding dean: Frans Bax, later president of CIA University. `CONFIRMED` — [CSI, "Sherman Kent and the Profession of Intelligence Analysis"](https://www.cia.gov/resources/csi/static/Kent-Profession-Intel-Analysis.pdf).
- **Joseph W. Gartin**, then Deputy Associate Director of CIA for Learning and a former Kent School dean, wrote "The Future of Analysis" in *Studies in Intelligence* — arguing for a future in which AI "sifts data, spots discontinuities, and synthesizes results; analysts provide theory and structure," and explicitly framing the challenge as responding "to the criticisms of Tetlock and others." Kent's own doctrine, per Gartin: "information is rigorously evaluated for validity (countering Denial & Deception) and **diagnosticity** (managing 'noise')… **Uncertainties and gaps in information are made explicit and accounted for in making predictions.**" `CONFIRMED` — [CSI, "The Future of Analysis"](https://www.cia.gov/resources/csi/static/Future-of-Analysis.pdf).

`INFERRED`: a senior CIA officer has published, in CIA's own journal, that (a) diagnosticity is core Kent doctrine, and (b) the IC must answer Tetlock-style accuracy criticism. Those are the two things your Brier/calibration package and your diagnosticity-weighted ACH do. The Kent School is the natural transition partner for training and for tradecraft-evaluation criteria, not the buyer of a system.

### 1.10 The analytic ombuds and the tradecraft review machinery

### 2.7 Forecasting, calibration and wargaming — the demonstrable gap

Nothing in the public record shows CIA running a measured forecasting-calibration program. The IC's forecasting research lineage sits at **IARPA**, and it is dormant:
- **ACE (Aggregative Contingent Estimation)** — "dramatically enhance the accuracy, precision, and timeliness of intelligence forecasts… empirical testing of forecasting accuracy against real events." `CONFIRMED` — [IARPA](https://www.iarpa.gov/research-programs/ace).
- **HFC (Hybrid Forecasting Competition)** — human+machine hybrid geopolitical forecasting; concluded May 2020; achievements included "Beat the state of the art for human-only forecast systems with a 10 percent increase in accuracy over baseline" and "Conducted foundational research on human trust of machine models." `CONFIRMED` — [IARPA](https://www.iarpa.gov/research-programs/hfc).
- **FOCUS (Forecasting Counterfactuals in Uncontrolled Settings)** — counterfactual reasoning; **"Because we can't re-run history in the real world, FOCUS methods were evaluated by having hundreds of people make predictions about outcomes in complex simulations."** `CONFIRMED` — [IARPA](https://www.iarpa.gov/research-programs/focus).
- **REASON (Rapid Explanation, Analysis and Sourcing Online)** — "enable intelligence analysts to substantially improve the **evidence and reasoning** in draft analytic reports… by pointing them to key pieces of evidence beyond what they have already considered and by helping them **determine which alternative explanations have the strongest support**… It will function in the same manner as an automated grammar checker but with a focus on evidence and reasoning." Performers were evaluated by an **independent test & evaluation team**. `CONFIRMED` — [IARPA](https://www.iarpa.gov/research-programs/reason).

`INFERRED`: **REASON is the closest prior art to your product in the entire USG record, and FOCUS is the closest prior art to your synthetic-oracle evaluation method** (evaluate against outcomes in complex simulations precisely because history cannot be re-run). Cite both. Citing REASON says you know the lineage; citing FOCUS pre-legitimizes synthetic-oracle evidence as an IC-accepted evaluation regime rather than a convenient substitute for real skill. Also note the difference honestly: REASON improved a human's draft; you produce the ensemble and the adjudication, and you *measure* the result.

## 4. The analytic standards the DA is held to

Statutory root: **50 U.S.C. § 3364** (from IRTPA §§ 1019–1020) requires the DNI to assign responsibility for ensuring finished intelligence is "timely, objective, independent of political considerations, based upon all sources of available intelligence, and employ the standards of proper analytic tradecraft," to perform regular detailed product reviews, and to **"annually conduct a survey of analytic objectivity."** Reviews must assess "whether the product… properly describe[s] the quality and reliability of underlying sources, properly caveat and express uncertainties or confidence in analytic judgments, properly distinguish between underlying intelligence and the assumptions and judgments of analysts, and **incorporate, where appropriate, alternative analyses**." Congress also requires an **annual report by 1 February** on ICD 203 compliance incidents reported to analytic ombudspersons, and a report on how ICD 203 compliance factors into **performance evaluations, merit pay, bonuses and promotions**. `CONFIRMED` — [50 U.S.C. § 3364](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/50/3364).

`INFERRED`: ICD 203 compliance is tied by statute to individual analysts' pay and promotion. That makes a tool that *demonstrably improves measured tradecraft compliance* a personnel-incentive-aligned purchase, not just a capability purchase. That argument is worth one sentence and no more.

### 4.1 ICD 203 — the five Analytic Standards and nine Tradecraft Standards

Signed 2 January 2015 by DNI James Clapper, superseding the 2007 version; technically amended 21 December 2022 to make the ODNI Analytic Ombuds head of the IC Analytic Ombuds Community of Practice and an independent entity reporting directly to the Deputy Director for Mission Integration (DD/MI). `CONFIRMED` — [ICD 203](https://www.intelligence.gov/assets/documents/intelligence-community-directives/ICD_203.pdf); [technical amendment text](https://www.bmbs.org/salamanca/readings/ODNI_ICDs_203-206-208.pdf).

**The five:** (a) Objective; (b) Independent of political consideration; (c) Timely; (d) Based on all available sources of intelligence information; (e) Implements and exhibits Analytic Tradecraft Standards.

**The nine Tradecraft Standards**, with the clauses your architecture implements structurally quoted verbatim:

**(1) Properly describes quality and credibility of underlying sources, data, and methodologies.** "Analytic products should identify underlying sources and methodologies upon which judgments are based, and use source descriptors in accordance with ICD 206… Source summary statements… are strongly encouraged and should be used to provide a holistic assessment of the strengths or weaknesses in the source base and explain which sources are most important to key analytic judgments."
→ *Your provenance-on-every-claim model and your unsupported-assertion rate. Note the standard is about the source **base**, not just individual citations — your source-quality tiering across the well-evidenced / medium / wide-prior / misspecified arms is a machine-generated source summary statement.*

**(2) Properly expresses and explains uncertainties associated with major analytic judgments.** "…specifically the **likelihood of occurrence of an event or development, and the analyst's confidence in the basis for this judgment**. Degrees of likelihood encompass a full spectrum from remote to nearly certain. Analysts' confidence in an assessment or judgment may be based on the logic and evidentiary base that underpin it, including the quantity and quality of source material, and their understanding of the topic… **As appropriate, products should identify indicators that would alter the levels of uncertainty for major analytic judgments.**"

The mandated likelihood lexicon, verbatim (two permitted rows, with bands):

| almost no chance | very unlikely | unlikely | roughly even chance | likely | very likely | almost certain(ly) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| remote | highly improbable | improbable (improbably) | roughly even odds | probable (probably) | highly probable | nearly certain |
| **01–05%** | **05–20%** | **20–45%** | **45–55%** | **55–80%** | **80–95%** | **95–99%** |

"Analysts are strongly encouraged not to mix terms from different rows. Products that do mix terms must include a disclaimer clearly noting the terms indicate the same assessment of probability."

**And the rule your type system enforces:**
> "**(b) To avoid confusion, products that express an analyst's confidence in an assessment or judgment using a 'confidence level' (e.g., 'high confidence') must not combine a confidence level and a degree of likelihood, which refers to an event or development, in the same sentence.**"

→ *This is the single clause to lead with. ICD 203 forbids collapsing likelihood and confidence at the level of a sentence; your architecture forbids it at the level of a type — there is structurally no function of type `(Judgment) => number`. That is a stronger guarantee than the directive asks for, and it is auditable. Also note the lexicon is a set of **bands**, exactly the representation your Framer already emits — your outcome bands are the ICD 203 lexicon's numeric form, not a translation of it.*

**(3) Properly distinguishes between underlying intelligence information and analysts' assumptions and judgments.** "Assumptions are defined as suppositions used to frame or support an argument… Judgments are defined as conclusions based on underlying intelligence information, analysis, and assumptions. **Products should state assumptions explicitly when they serve as the linchpin of an argument or when they bridge key information gaps. Products should explain the implications for judgments if assumptions prove to be incorrect. Products also should, as appropriate, identify indicators that, if detected, would alter judgments.**"
→ *Your key-assumptions check with a **required falsifier** per assumption is the mechanical form of "explain the implications if assumptions prove incorrect" + "identify indicators that would alter judgments." The directive says "as appropriate"; you make it mandatory and machine-checked.*

**(4) Incorporates analysis of alternatives.** "Analysis of alternatives is the systematic evaluation of differing hypotheses to explain events or phenomena, explore near-term outcomes, and imagine possible futures to mitigate surprise and risk. Analytic products should identify and assess **plausible alternative hypotheses**. This is particularly important when major judgments must contend with significant uncertainties, or complexity (e.g., **forecasting future trends**), or when **low probability events could produce high-impact results**. In discussing alternatives, products should address factors such as associated assumptions, likelihood, or implications related to U.S. interests. Products also should identify indicators that, if detected, would affect the likelihood of identified alternatives."
→ *Your Framer's N mutually-exclusive, exhaustive outcome bands are alternatives whose mutual exclusivity is **structural rather than asserted** — a property a free-text alternatives section can never have. And "low probability events could produce high-impact results" is your tail-recall metric, named by the directive.*

**(5) Demonstrates customer relevance and addresses implications.** "…add value by addressing prospects, context, threats, or factors affecting opportunities for action."

**(6) Uses clear and logical argumentation.** "Analytic products should present a **clear main analytic message up front**… Products should be internally consistent and **acknowledge significant supporting and contrary information affecting judgments**."
→ *BLUF; and your contradiction precision/recall metric measures compliance with the second clause.*

**(7) Explains change to or consistency of analytic judgments.** "…should state how their major judgments on a topic are consistent with or represent a change from those in previously published analysis… **They should avoid using boilerplate language**… **Significant differences in analytic judgment, such as between two IC analytic elements, should be fully considered and brought to the attention of customers.**"
→ *Inter-run stability and Krippendorff alpha are quantified versions of both clauses.*

**(8) Makes accurate judgments and assessments.** "Analytic products should apply expertise and logic to make the most accurate judgments and assessments possible, based on the information available and known information gaps. In doing so, **analytic products should present all judgments that would be useful to customers, and should not avoid difficult judgments in order to minimize the risk of being wrong.**… analytic products should express judgments as clearly and precisely as possible, reducing ambiguity by addressing the likelihood, timing, and nature of the outcome or development. **Clarity of meaning permits assessment for accuracy when all necessary information is available.**"

⚠️ **This is the one standard your architecture is in tension with, and you must address it head-on.** "Should not avoid difficult judgments in order to minimize the risk of being wrong" is the tradecraft objection to `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` as a first-class verdict. `INFERRED`, and this is the argument I would make: the standard forbids *hedging to avoid being scored*, and your **sharpness** metric is precisely a hedging detector. The standard does not forbid *declaring a question unanswerable on the available evidence* — it forbids ducking. The distinction is empirical, and you have the empirical answer: false-abstention and true-abstention rates are measured, the gate fires 80/80 on a provably-unanswerable arm and 0–7/80 on answerable ones. **An unmeasured abstention is hedging; a measured one with a published false-abstention rate is a judgment about the evidence base.** Say this explicitly, in the white paper, with the numbers. Do not let a reviewer discover the tension first.

**(9) Incorporates effective visual information where appropriate.** "…visual presentations should be used when information or concepts… can be conveyed better in graphic form… **Analytic content in visual information should also adhere to other analytic tradecraft standards.**"
→ *Reliability diagrams and ECE/MCE plots — but note the last sentence: your charts inherit the tradecraft obligations, including sourcing.*

### 4.2 ICD 206 — Sourcing Requirements

Signed 22 January 2015. `CONFIRMED` — [ICD 206](https://www.intelligence.gov/assets/documents/intelligence-community-directives/ICD_206.pdf).

Purpose: "Sourcing information shall be included in covered analytic products to **enhance the credibility and transparency of intelligence analysis, and to assist readers in making an informed assessment of the quality and scope of sources** underlying the analysis… Sourcing information shall also be structured to enable assessment of the use and utility of sourcing."

Four mechanisms: **Source Reference Citation (SRC)**, **Appended Reference Citation (ARC)**, **source descriptor**, **source summary statement**.

**The clause that maps one-to-one onto your provenance model:**
> "An SRC shall be generated each time a source is directly cited in product text (e.g., 'according to…') or **a specific aspect of analysis (i.e., judgment, assessment, estimate, alternative hypothesis or view, confidence level) depends on a source.**"

→ *ICD 206 explicitly names **alternative hypothesis** and **confidence level** as things that must carry a citation when they depend on a source. Your per-claim provenance is not an extra; it is the directive's own object model. Your unsupported-assertion **rate** is a direct measure of SRC coverage.*

Required SRC elements (nine): portion marking; originator/author/producer/owner; **an unambiguous source identifier** enabling "unambiguous and efficient location and retrieval"; title; **date of issuance, publication, or posting — "If date of online posting is unknown, then date of online access"**; page number; classification of extracted information; overall classification of the source document; source descriptor.

Source summary statement: "should cover **strengths and weaknesses of the source base, which sources are most important to key judgments, what sources are meaningfully corroborative or conflicting**, and should highlight any specific subject matter expertise used to develop the assessment."

**Retention — the clause that makes your as-of corpus a compliance artifact:**
> "If citing a source that is **dynamic (e.g., from an Internet posting), ephemeral… or not subject to an IC element's policy for systematic storage** for analyst access… **a record of the source shall be preserved for retention** by the IC element producing the covered analytic product **for at least one year**. For products with a specified time horizon… applicable source information shall be retained for the period of the time horizon or **five years, whichever is shorter**."

→ *Your as-of corpus retrieval with a hard date filter, plus the runtime assertion that throws if any evidence reference post-dates the run's as-of date, produces exactly the retained, date-stamped record ICD 206 mandates — and it produces it automatically rather than as an analyst's clerical burden. Frame the leakage guard as **both** a scientific control **and** an ICD 206 retention/date-integrity mechanism. That double framing is worth real money to the buyer.*

Also: "In covered analytic products that include a tearline, SRCs should be inserted after the body of the report and before the tearline version," and downgraded/sanitized copies "should contain sourcing information." Exemptions must be requested in writing from DD/MI. Products issued without full sourcing under crisis conditions must be **re-issued with full sourcing within 30 days**.

### 4.3 ICD 208 — Maximizing the Utility of Analytic Products

Supersedes the 2008 "Write for Maximum Utility." `CONFIRMED` — [ICD 208](https://www.intelligence.gov/assets/documents/intelligence-community-directives/ICD_208.pdf).

Five principles, **in a stated hierarchy — "the first principle is foremost in consideration and the last principle the least"**:
1. **Know Your Customers** — "Analysts should determine prior to publication whether their analytic product is focused on a specific customer or a general audience, which will help determine classification limits and sources used."
2. **Meet Analytic Standards** — products "provide customers with insight into the quality and credibility of underlying sources, characterize the uncertainty surrounding the analytic judgments… and include context that aids the customer's understanding."
3. **Produce for Tailored Re-Use** — tearlines at the lowest classification level; ICD 206 sourcing throughout; and the critical constraint:
   > "**Sanitized products should never render facts or judgments in a manner inconsistent with their higher-classified version; facts, judgments, confidence levels, and probabilistic language must be congruent.**"
4. **Make Analytic Products Discoverable and Accessible** — at minimum, disseminate electronically to the **Library of National Intelligence**.
5. **Demonstrate Transparency** — follow the Principles of Intelligence Transparency.

→ *Principle 3's congruence rule is a machine-checkable invariant on a structured product and an unenforceable aspiration on prose. A system whose likelihood band, confidence field, and provenance set are first-class data can **prove** that a tearline is congruent with its parent. `INFERRED`, but it is a strong, specific, differentiating claim, and no summarizer can make it.*

### 4.5 The known academic critique — cite it, don't hide from it

## 5. What a "white paper" means to this buyer

### 5.1 The institutional form

A white paper (used interchangeably with "abstract" or "concept paper" across agencies) is **stage one of a two-step screen**: a short document a government technical reviewer uses to decide whether to invite a full proposal. It is not a proposal, is not scored against other submissions, and — in most BAAs — an unfavorable review does **not** bar you from submitting a full proposal anyway. `CONFIRMED` — [IARPA BAA-15-04 §3.1.2](https://research-vp.tau.ac.il/sites/resauth.tau.ac.il/files/IARPA-BAA-15-04-Office-for-Anticipating-Surprise.pdf), [AcqNotes](https://acqnotes.com/acqnote/careerfields/broad-agency-announcements).

Important IARPA subtlety: "Neither prior discussions with offerors regarding their proposed idea **nor comments resulting from the review of an abstract** submitted prior to the offeror's submission of a proposal will be considered in the proposal's evaluation." `CONFIRMED` — same. The white paper buys you feedback and a control number, not credit.

If the actual solicitation specifies otherwise, its instructions win absolutely; non-conformance is the cheapest way to get binned.

### 5.3 The Heilmeier questions — write to these five

IARPA's stated criterion: "The proposal clearly articulates **quantitatively substantiated** answers to each of the Heilmeier questions." `CONFIRMED` — [IARPA Incisive Analysis BAA](https://info.publicintelligence.net/IARPA-IncisiveAnalysis.pdf). Verbatim:

1. What are you trying to do?
2. How is it done at present? Who does it? What are the limitations of present approaches?
3. What is new about your approach? Why do you think that you can be successful at this time?
4. If you succeed, what difference will it make?
5. How long will it take? How much will it cost? **How will you evaluate progress during and at the conclusion of the effort? (i.e., what are your proposed milestones and metrics?)**

`INFERRED`: question 5 is the one most submissions fumble and the one you are uniquely built to answer — you arrive with a metrics package (Brier, Brier skill vs explicit baselines, Murphy decomposition, ECE/MCE, sharpness, log score, coverage@N, ensemble diversity, tail recall, inter-run stability, Krippendorff alpha, contradiction P/R, provenance completeness, unsupported-assertion rate, cost per insight), an evaluation-regime tag on every report, and a rule that no function pools regimes. Lead the white paper's evaluation section with the regime tag — `synthetic_oracle | historical_backtest | live_post_cutoff` — because it *is* the honest answer to Heilmeier 5 and it pre-empts the "you're overclaiming" reflex.

### 5.4 Evaluation criteria and what gets binned

IARPA's technical criteria, in descending order of importance (typical BAA):
1. **Overall Scientific and Technical Merit** — "The technical approach is **credible, innovative, and concisely delineated with a clear assessment of primary risks and means to mitigate them**. Innovation will be judged in the context of the current state of the art."
2. **Effectiveness of the Proposed Work Plan** — "efficient, in that it is **targeted to demonstrate concept feasibility as quickly and cost effectively as possible**. The offeror's approach to achieving **quantifiable milestones** is explicitly described and substantiated… The proposed schedule is realistic and **critical paths are identified**."
3. **Contribution and Relevance to the IARPA Mission** — includes: "**The proposed approach to intellectual property rights is in the best interest of the Government.**"
4. **Relevant Experience and Expertise**
5. **Cost Realism** — "Estimates are 'realistic' when they are **neither excessive nor insufficient**… IARPA recognizes that undue emphasis on cost may motivate offerors to offer low-risk ideas and to staff the effort with junior personnel in order to be competitive. **IARPA discourages such cost strategies.**"

Elsewhere: "Evaluators will also assess the extent to which **the proposed intellectual property restrictions (if any) will impact the Government's ability to utilize and transition the technology to partners.**"

`CONFIRMED` — [IARPA-BAA-15-04](https://research-vp.tau.ac.il/sites/resauth.tau.ac.il/files/IARPA-BAA-15-04-Office-for-Anticipating-Surprise.pdf), [IARPA Incisive Analysis BAA](https://info.publicintelligence.net/IARPA-IncisiveAnalysis.pdf), [IARPA-BAA-21-02 MicroE4AI §5.1](https://imlive.s3.amazonaws.com/Federal%20Government/ID111023706858306338020324902621643587514/IARPA-BAA-21-02%20MicroE4AI%204%2030%2021%20%20FINAL%20C.pdf).

**Binned, per the documents themselves:**
- **Non-responsive / out of scope.** IARPA screens for mission relevance *before* technical evaluation: "If it is determined that a proposal is not relevant to the IARPA mission, the proposal will not be considered further for award." `CONFIRMED`.
- **Over the page limit or off-format.** "Abstracts that do not conform to these requirements may be rejected without review"; NIST: pages beyond the maximum "will not be evaluated"; and the all-caps standing warning: **"OFFERORS ARE CAUTIONED THAT EVALUATION RATINGS MAY BE LOWERED OR PROPOSALS REJECTED IF SUBMITTAL INSTRUCTIONS ARE NOT FOLLOWED."** `CONFIRMED`.
- **Incomplete.** NIST: "White Papers deemed ineligible, incomplete, or non-responsive will receive **no further consideration**." `CONFIRMED`.
- **No quantified milestones or metrics.** Failure of Heilmeier 5 and of criterion 2. `CONFIRMED` as criteria; `INFERRED` as the modal failure.
- **IP restrictions that impair government use or transition.** An explicit scored criterion. `CONFIRMED`.
- **Duplicative of existing work.** IARPA screens for "whether the proposal is not addressed by emerging or ongoing IARPA programs or other published IARPA solicitations." Army SMDC's rating language similarly rewards "minimal duplication with ongoing or completed work." `CONFIRMED`.
- **Cost-shaved to win.** IARPA explicitly discourages it. `CONFIRMED`.

Useful timing note: some BAAs run **rolling white paper review with a ~15-day response target**, returning "encouraged" or "not encouraged," and may "prioritize proposals that were encouraged by the Government during the white paper stage" — while still permitting the discouraged to proceed. `CONFIRMED` — [IARPA-BAA-21-02](https://imlive.s3.amazonaws.com/Federal%20Government/ID111023706858306338020324902621643587514/IARPA-BAA-21-02%20MicroE4AI%204%2030%2021%20%20FINAL%20C.pdf).

### 5.5 Recommended five-page skeleton for this specific buyer

`INFERRED` throughout — this is my recommendation, not a sourced requirement. Every element traces to something cited above.

**Page 1 — Problem and claim.**
- One sentence naming the buyer's own words: the DA's stated novel use case is "simulating crisis scenarios" (McCool, Feb 2024) and the Deputy Director has committed to AI coworkers that "compare drafts against tradecraft standards" within two years (Ellis, Apr 2026).
- The gap in one sentence: the IC has never instrumented ICD 203's third premise — that good reasoning yields accurate judgments (LSE study). Osiris solved summarization; nothing public measures calibration, abstention, or leakage.
- The claim with its evidence class, first line, unhedged: **synthetic oracle only, 15,832 simulations; Brier skill vs base rate tracks evidence quality monotonically (+0.44 / +0.42 / +0.11 / −0.46); abstention gate fires 80/80 on a provably-unanswerable arm, 0–7/80 on answerable ones; real-world forecasting skill is not yet measured and the system says so in the first line of every run.** Do not bury this. Volunteering the limit is the credibility move at an agency whose own journal calls LLM products "brittle, untested, and untrustworthy."

**Page 2 — Approach, with the ICD mapping as a table.** Framer→ICD 203 (4) + the likelihood lexicon bands; deterministic seeded kernel→ICD 503 accreditability and reproducibility; abstention gate before scoring→the standard-8 tension, answered with measured false-abstention rates; adjudication panel→the CIA Tradecraft Primer's own ACH/KAC/devil's advocacy/premortem/red-team set; calibration layer→confidence aggregates by weakest link; product→ICD 203 (2)(b) separation enforced at type level, ICD 206 SRC object model, ICD 208 congruence.

**Page 3 — Evaluation.** The metrics package, the mandatory regime tag, the no-pooling rule, the two leakage guards (as-of hard date filter + runtime assertion that throws on post-as-of references; synthetic closed worlds whose ground truth did not exist before the run). State the roadmap: synthetic oracle → historical backtest → live post-cutoff, with the gate criteria for each transition. This is Heilmeier 5.

**Page 4 — Answering the buyer's own objections, by name.** Quote Brown's "brittle, untested, and untrustworthy… whose inner workings not even their designers can entirely explain" and the *Road Ahead* black-box passage, and answer each with a mechanism: byte-identical artifact hashes; the math simulates and the LLM only frames and interprets; unsupported assertions counted and reported rather than silently suppressed; model-agnostic by construction, which is also the answer to "the whims of a single company."

**Page 5 — Work plan, transition, cost, IP.** Quantifiable milestones with critical path; named transition partners (DA analytic-innovation line for the tradecraft product, DMS for enterprise hosting on a C2E fabric, CCI for the adversary-cyber scenario class, Kent School for tradecraft-evaluation criteria); explicit, permissive government-purpose-rights posture stated up front; realistic cost with no shaving.

**What to leave out.** Any model vendor's name. Any claim of real-world forecasting skill. Any positioning as summarization or search. Any speculation about classified programs. Any page six.
